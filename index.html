Shop Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Shop Manager is an offline business record-keeping application. This policy explains what information users may enter, how the app stores it, and how backup works.

In brief: Shop Manager does not require an account or login. The app is designed to keep business records on the device. The app does not include an app-operated online account or cloud service.

1. Information you enter

Depending on how you use the app, you may enter shop details such as shop name, email address, phone number, tax registration number (TRN), address and logo. You may also enter sales, purchases, expenses, payment methods, transaction dates, notes, currency preferences and related business records.

2. Where information is stored

Information entered into Shop Manager is stored in the app's local data storage on your device. The app does not require registration and does not provide an app-operated online account or server for uploading your business records.

3. Data sharing and advertising

The app source reviewed for this release does not declare the Android Internet permission and does not include advertising or analytics SDK dependencies. The app developer does not receive the business records you enter through an app-operated online service.

If you independently share a backup file or other information outside the app, the recipient's services and privacy terms will apply.

4. Backup and restore

Android automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for Shop Manager. The app provides manual backup and restore features. When creating a backup, you choose the destination using the Android file selection interface. If you choose a cloud storage provider or share the file with another person, that provider's or recipient's privacy practices apply.

Keep backup files secure. Anyone who obtains an unprotected backup file may be able to access its contents.

5. Permissions and offline operation

Shop Manager is designed for offline use. Its core business-record features do not require an internet connection. Android may show system interfaces when you select a file location for manual backup or restore.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your records remain in the app's local storage until you delete them using app features or remove the app's data or uninstall the app. Make a manual backup before changing devices or removing the app. Deleting a record in the app does not delete copies previously exported or backed up elsewhere.

7. Children's privacy

Shop Manager is a general business utility and is not designed specifically for children.

8. Security

Protect your device with a screen lock and keep backup files in a secure location. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published at the policy URL shown in the app's Google Play listing.
